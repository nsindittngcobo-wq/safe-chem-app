# Sample Assessment: Household Chlorine Bleach

This example shows the expected output format when an agent assesses a household hypochlorite bleach product using `SKILL.md`.

---

## Risk Level: HIGH

### 1. Product & Chemical Profile
- **Identified Product:** Household Thick Bleach (e.g., 5% Sodium Hypochlorite Cleaner)
- **Key Active Ingredients:** Sodium hypochlorite (typically ~3%–6% w/w), sodium hydroxide (trace/pH stabilizer), surfactants
- **Primary Hazards:** Corrosive to eyes and skin; causes respiratory irritation if vapors accumulate; hazardous if mixed with acids or ammonia

### 2. Immediate First-Aid Actions
- **Eye Contact:** Hold eyelids open and flush gently with cool or lukewarm running water for at least 15–20 minutes. Remove contact lenses after the first few minutes if easily removable and continue flushing. Seek urgent medical care if pain, redness, or vision changes persist.
- **Skin Contact:** Remove contaminated clothing/jewelry and wash the affected area thoroughly with soap and running water for at least 15 minutes. Seek medical attention if irritation or burns develop.
- **Inhalation:** Move immediately to fresh air away from fumes or enclosed spaces. Loosen tight clothing and monitor breathing; seek immediate medical help if coughing, wheezing, shortness of breath, or chest pain occurs.
- **Ingestion:** Do **not** induce vomiting unless explicitly instructed by a poison control center or medical professional. Rinse mouth with water and give a small sip of water if the person is alert and able to swallow without gagging; contact poison control or emergency services immediately.

### 3. Critical Do's & Don'ts (Incompatibilities)
- **NEVER Mix With:** Acids (vinegar, toilet bowl cleaners, descalers, citric/hydrochloric acid — releases toxic **chlorine gas**), ammonia or ammonium cleaners (releases toxic **chloramine gas**), rubbing alcohol/acetone (can form **chloroform** and hazardous byproducts), or hydrogen peroxide.
- **Safe Handling / PPE:** Use in a well-ventilated area; wear chemical-resistant gloves and eye protection, especially when pouring or scrubbing surfaces.

> **Medical Disclaimer:** This guidance is for immediate informational triage only and does not replace professional medical care. In an emergency, contact your local Poison Control Center or emergency medical services immediately and keep the product container nearby.
