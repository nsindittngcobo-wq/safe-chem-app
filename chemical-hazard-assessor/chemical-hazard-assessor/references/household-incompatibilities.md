# Common Household Chemical Incompatibilities & Reactive Pairs

Use this reference when evaluating whether two or more household products, cleaners, or chemicals can be mixed, used in the same poorly ventilated space, or applied sequentially on a surface.

> **Safety Rule:** Never mix household cleaning products unless a manufacturer label explicitly states they are compatible. When in doubt, treat the combination as **HIGH RISK**.

---

## High-Danger Reactive Combinations

| Combination | Primary Hazard / Reaction Product | Immediate Risk & Symptoms |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Chlorine Bleach (Sodium Hypochlorite) + Ammonia** | **Chloramine gas** (and hydrazine at elevated concentrations) | Severe respiratory irritation, coughing, shortness of breath, chest pain, watery/burning eyes, nausea; can cause pulmonary edema in confined spaces. |
| **Chlorine Bleach + Acids** (vinegar, citric acid, toilet bowl cleaners, rust removers, muriatic/hydrochloric acid) | **Chlorine gas** | Choking, intense burning of eyes, nose, and throat, wheezing, chest tightness; potentially fatal at high concentrations in enclosed areas. |
| **Chlorine Bleach + Rubbing Alcohol (Isopropyl Alcohol) or Acetone** | **Chloroform** and other chlorinated/oxidized byproducts | Dizziness, lightheadedness, central nervous system depression, nausea, eye/respiratory irritation, and potential organ toxicity with heavy exposure. |
| **Chlorine Bleach + Hydrogen Peroxide** | **Oxygen gas + chlorates / heat** | Rapid decomposition releasing oxygen and heat; pressurized containers can rupture or splash corrosive liquid. |
| **Hydrogen Peroxide + Vinegar (Acetic Acid)** | **Peracetic acid** (formed in mixture) | Strong irritant to eyes, skin, and upper respiratory tract; worsens with higher concentrations or poor ventilation. |
| **Strong Acids + Strong Alkalis / Drain Cleaners** (e.g., hydrochloric/sulfuric acid + sodium hydroxide) | **Exothermic reaction (intense heat) + corrosive splash/steam** | Violent boiling or splattering of caustic/acidic liquid, thermal burns plus chemical burns, and hazardous fumes. |
| **Chlorine Bleach + Organic Matter / Certain Pesticides or Sanitizers** | **Toxic chlorinated vapors / unpredictable reactions** | Irritation of airways and eyes; avoid mixing with quaternary ammonium compounds ("quats") or unverified disinfectants unless specified. |

---

## Triage Rules for Mixing & Sequential Use

1. **Prioritize Toxic Gas & Vapor Alerts:** When a user asks about mixing bleach with acids, ammonia, or solvents, put the **HIGH RISK** warning and toxic gas name (**chlorine gas**, **chloramine gas**, or **chloroform**) right at the top of the response.
2. **Account for Residue on Surfaces:** Even if two products are not poured into the same bucket, applying a second cleaner onto a surface with wet or dried residue (such as toilet bowls, sinks, or countertops) can still trigger a reaction.
3. **Ventilation First:** If fumes or vapors are already present, advise moving immediately to fresh air and avoiding enclosed spaces—never attempt to "neutralize" a chemical spill or gas release with another household chemical.
4. **Emergency Escalation:** Instruct the user to contact local poison control or emergency medical services right away if someone experiences breathing difficulty, chest pain, persistent coughing, severe eye/skin burning, or confusion/dizziness.
